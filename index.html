The loop

Fail on the robot. Dream the fix. Verify in the twin. Train. Repeat.

실기 실패에서 출발해 세계모델이 장면을 상상하고, 트윈에서 물리적으로 걸러, 학습으로 돌아오는 폐루프.

  1. Start from the robot's own failuresNot random augmentation. The initial frame of every dream is a state where the policy actually failed.
  2. Imagine, then attach actionsCosmos Predict dreams the scene forward; an inverse-dynamics model writes the 22-dof actions back.
  3. Keep only what physics allowsThe twin replays each dream. Joint limits, collisions, unreachable grasps are thrown out before training.
  4. Co-train and go againReal data plus verified dreams become the next checkpoint, deployed to the on-robot Thor.
